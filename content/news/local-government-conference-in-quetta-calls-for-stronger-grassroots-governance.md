**QUETTA, March 1, 2025:** In a significant development for local governance in Balochistan, the Local Councils Association of Balochistan (LCA Balochistan), in collaboration with the Association for Development of Local Governance (ADLG), organized a Local Government Conference in Quetta, bringing elected local government representatives together with the province’s political leadership and senior government officials.

The conference provided mayors, district council chairmen and other elected representatives with a direct platform to discuss challenges facing local governments with Balochistan Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti, Provincial Minister Mir Ali Hassan Zehri and other senior officials.

The provincial leadership announced a number of measures aimed at strengthening the financial and administrative position of local governments. These included an increase in local government funds to **Rs33 billion**, greater tax collection authority for municipal committees, and salaries and benefits for district council chairmen and heads of municipal bodies.

The conference also addressed concerns regarding the security of elected local representatives and their democratic mandate. The provincial leadership assured participants that elected local government representatives would not be removed through undemocratic means.

A key outcome of the conference was the signing of a **Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between ADLG and the Government of Balochistan**. The agreement establishes a framework for collaboration on strengthening local governance through training and capacity building, institutional reforms and policy advocacy.

The conference marked an important step in creating a direct channel of engagement between Balochistan’s provincial leadership and elected representatives at the local level. The participation of senior provincial officials and commitments announced during the event underscored the growing importance of empowered and financially sustainable local governments in delivering services and responding to community needs.

LCA Balochistan and ADLG reaffirmed their commitment to continuing advocacy and institutional engagement for stronger, more effective and democratic local governance across the province.
