7 July 2026 (Islamabad): A meaningful gathering took place at the ADLG Office in Islamabad today, as Mayors and District Council Chairmen from across Azad Jammu & Kashmir sat down to chart a better future for their communities.

The meeting centred on establishing a Local Council Association (LCA) for AJ&K, a body that would bring local governance closer to the people. The goal is straightforward: stronger institutions, better municipal services in every district, and more opportunities for ordinary citizens at the grassroots level.
